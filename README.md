# The Last Thing She Remembered
### A Mina Dawaar Novel

A court reporter survives a strangulation in a courthouse parking deck during the worst snowstorm in Pennsylvania history, and loses the five minutes before she blacked out. While the whole town waits for those minutes to come back, someone is running out the clock on the only record that matters — the transcript she typed with her own hands.
