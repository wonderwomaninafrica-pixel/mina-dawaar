The Pale Room

The heat is real even when the lover might not be.

Mina has a comfortable marriage, a quiet apartment, and an app that was never meant to find her anyone.

The match comes back at ninety-four percent. He is not looking either. He answers at two in the morning, every time, within minutes, and he listens to her in a way she has not been listened to in a very long time.

She tells herself it's only conversation, right up until the afternoon she books the room. Thursdays, three o'clock, the same door on the third floor.

By the seventh month she is not eating, not sleeping, wearing long sleeves in warm rooms, lying to everyone who loves her. Her husband has stopped asking. Her closest friend has stopped calling.

There is one afternoon a week in which she is entirely present — and six days walking around the outside of her own life, waiting.

She would not give it back, even if she could never prove any of it happened.

Contains explicit sexual content and depictions of addiction, obsession, and psychological distress.
